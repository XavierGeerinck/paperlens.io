direction A"the TUI" — chosen

The thesis

An AI engineer's two native artifacts are the arXiv PDF and the terminal. The current site borrows the generic sci-fi HUD look (zinc, indigo accent, uppercase fake status labels). Direction A commits to the terminal fully: the site behaves like a TUI you already know how to drive — ranger for browsing, glow for reading, tmux for chrome, vim for keys.

The signature

The ranger-style two-pane browser on the home page: a dense, scannable list on the left, a live preview on the right, driven by j/k/⏎. Cards are gone; 28 entries fit on one screen and the preview carries the imagery. Everything else stays quiet so this one element is what people remember.

What "nerdy" buys the reader

Density without clutter (a whole index above the fold), a keyboard path through the whole site, honest labels (git log, whoami, :toc), and a simulation that sits in the reading flow as a split pane instead of a hero image.

The one risk taken

Monospace body text for 15–25 minute reads. Set at 15px / 1.75 on a 76ch measure it reads well in testing, and it is what the audience reads docs in all day. If it proves tiring, the fallback is IBM Plex Serif for prose only — the chrome stays mono and nothing else changes.

direction B"the Preprint" — not chosen, kept for reference

The thesis

The home page is page 1 of an arXiv preprint, viewed in a PDF viewer. Two justified columns, Times-like STIX, the rotated arXiv:… stamp, a \marginpar, and the exact hyperref link-box colours (red internal, green citations, cyan URLs). The index is the reference list: entries are [1]–[28].

Why it might win

It is the more surprising joke, and it reads beautifully on paper-white. Long-form prose is at home here in a way it has to argue for in Direction A.

Why it is the alternative

The simulations — the product — look like an intruder on a printed page, and every interactive control has to be explained away. It is single-theme by nature. And a strict two-column layout collapses to a plain single column on phones, losing most of its character.

open questionsfor review

  • Mono prose — keep it, or switch the article body to IBM Plex Serif? Read article.html end to end before deciding.
  • Game of Life — drop, or keep behind an easter egg?
  • Categories — the three home sections (My Thoughts / Deep Dive / Papers) become one list with a filter. Fine, or do you want them visually separated?
  • Featured — one featured entry with its live mini-sim, versus the current single featured card. Multiple featured: true entries exist in the content; the browser could pin them with a ★ instead.
