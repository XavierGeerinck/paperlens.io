PaperLens brand

a research site that looks like a terminal — because that's where the readers already live.

PaperLens rebuilds the core mechanism of a paper as something you can drag a slider on. The brand should feel like a well-configured terminal: quiet, dense, precise, and a little bit fun. Nothing glows. Nothing is centred. Colour means something.

dark only

one typeface, one primary colour, four semantic hues

every screen drivable from the keyboard

logothree lockups, one idea: a prompt

vocabulary · maturity is a chip, type is a coloured word. These are the only two labels an entry gets.

do

  • lowercase labels, sentence-case prose
  • name things by what they are: git log, whoami, :toc
  • say the number: "28 entries", not "many"
  • errors say what happened and what to do next

don't

  • fake status ("SYSTEM: NORMAL", "clearance")
  • uppercase hero copy, exclamation marks
  • colour as decoration — no gradients, no glow
  • emoji as section markers (one 🔥 for a streak is the limit)

motionone orchestrated moment per page

bootThe prompt types its command (steps, ~0.45 s), then output appears line by line at 100–150 ms intervals. Home and 404 only.
demo$ ls ~/papers --sort=date
cursorBlinks at 1.1 s, steps(1). Only where input is possible.
selectionInstant. No easing on row selection or tab changes — a terminal doesn't tween.
simulationsRun at 60 fps; the only continuous motion on a page. Pause when off-screen.
reduced motionBoot renders fully, cursor stops, simulations show their final frame with a play control.

layoutleft-aligned, dense, 1280 max

gridContent column 1280 px max, 16 px gutters. Panes carry a 1 px bg2 border and a 6 px radius; chips 4 px.
density28 rows fit above the fold on a 1440×900 screen. Row height 30 px. If it needs a card, it probably needs a row.
measureProse 76ch at 15 px. Figures (code, diagrams, tables) may take the full column.
chromeTitle bar 38 px on top, status bar 28 px at the bottom, on every page including 404.
mobilePanes stack, the preview pane drops below the list, key hints hide. Nothing centres.
